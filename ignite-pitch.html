How It Works End-to-End

The Complete Ignite Workflow

1

Prospect Discovers Your Website

A buyer finds your branded property site through a referral, social media, or search. The site is fast, beautiful, and loaded with relevant local listings for your market. A session tracker begins recording the moment they arrive — invisibly and without interrupting their experience.

2

They Browse, Calculate, and Engage

As the visitor clicks through MLS-connected listings, Ignite logs each one. If they open the mortgage calculator and adjust the sliders, that behavior is captured as a financial intent signal. Their total time on site is tracked. Every meaningful action is recorded silently in real time.

3

They Submit the Contact Form

When the prospect decides to reach out, they fill in their name, phone, email, intent (buying, selling, renting), budget range, and an optional message. On submission, their entire behavioral session is packaged with this contact information and written to your secure database.

4

Ignite Scores the Lead and Triggers the AI

Within seconds, Ignite calculates the intent score from all captured signals. Simultaneously, the Google Gemini AI engine reads the prospect's full profile — their browsing behavior, calculator usage, budget, and message — and composes a personalized first-contact message specifically for this person.

5

You Receive an Instant Email Alert

An alert email lands in your inbox within seconds. It contains the prospect's full contact details, a summary of everything they did on site, and the AI-generated suggested opener. You have everything you need to make a high-quality first call without opening a single app.

6

You Act from the CRM on Any Device

Open your Ignite CRM from your phone or laptop. The new lead appears at the top of Priority Contacts if their intent score ranks them there. Click into their profile to see the full behavioral timeline, hit Initiate to reveal the AI SmartDraft, then tap Text or Email to send it in one action.

7

Scores Evolve. Priority Contacts Stay Current.

As leads age or new ones come in, intent scores update automatically. The Priority Contacts dashboard always reflects the current top five — so you always know where your energy is best spent, no matter how many leads are in the system.
